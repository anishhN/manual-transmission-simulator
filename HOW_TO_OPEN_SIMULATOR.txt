TRANSMISSION DYNAMICS STUDIO — UPDATED BUILD

Published Yanmar 2GM20F engine specifications: 0.636 L, 75 x 72 mm bore/stroke, 23:1 compression, 11.8 kW at 3400 rpm continuous, and 13.4 kW at 3600 rpm one-hour rating.
